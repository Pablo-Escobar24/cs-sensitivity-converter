The conversion multiplies by Call of Duty yaw (0.0066) divided by CS 1.6 yaw (0.022), a factor of 0.3. That keeps the mouse distance for a full turn identical.

Before you rely on it

  • A Call of Duty sensitivity of 6 becomes 1.8 in CS 1.6 at the same DPI.
  • Set m_rawinput 1 in CS 1.6 and keep Windows pointer precision off, so the converted value is not distorted.
  • CS 1.6 accepts decimals through the console, for example sensitivity 1.8, which is more precise than the menu slider.

How to check it worked

Put a mark on your mousepad, turn your character one full 360° in a practice map in each game, and compare the distance. They should match within a few millimetres. If they do not, check that mouse acceleration is off and that DPI is the same.
