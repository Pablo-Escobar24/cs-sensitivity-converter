PUBG sensitivity calibrator

Match PUBG to the game you aim best in, using a measured distance instead of an unreliable formula.

PUBG’s sensitivity slider is not a simple linear scale that sources agree on, and it can also change with field of view and scope. Published yaw values for PUBG disagree with each other, so a formula would give you a wrong number. This page uses a method that works regardless: find the cm/360 you want from another game, then tune PUBG until it matches.

Step 2: measure PUBG and get your next value

In the training ground, use the same mode you play (for example third-person hipfire) and the same DPI. Turn your character one full 360° and measure how far the mouse moved.

Why repeat it

The suggestion assumes a straight-line relationship, which PUBG’s slider may not follow exactly. Enter the new value, measure again, and repeat until your measured distance is within about 2% of the target. Two or three rounds usually do it. Measure each scope separately, since they have their own sliders.
