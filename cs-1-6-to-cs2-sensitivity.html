CS 1.6 to CS2 sensitivity converter

Type your CS 1.6 sensitivity and get the CS2 value that turns your view the same distance.

CS 1.6 and CS2 both use a yaw of 0.022, so the number you type is the same in both games. A sensitivity of 2.5 in CS 1.6 is 2.5 in CS2.

The conversion multiplies by CS 1.6 yaw (0.022) divided by CS2 yaw (0.022), a factor of 1. That keeps the mouse distance for a full turn identical.

Before you rely on it

  • Your hand travels the same distance for a 360° turn, but the screen can still feel different because of field of view, resolution and stretched 4:3 settings.
  • In CS 1.6, turn on raw input with m_rawinput 1 and turn off Windows pointer precision, so the number you carry over is not changed by mouse acceleration.
  • Scoped sensitivity is handled separately in each game (see zoom_sensitivity_ratio), so check AWP and scout feel after you switch.

How to check it worked

Put a mark on your mousepad, turn your character one full 360° in a practice map in each game, and compare the distance. They should match within a few millimetres. If they do not, check that mouse acceleration is off and that DPI is the same.
